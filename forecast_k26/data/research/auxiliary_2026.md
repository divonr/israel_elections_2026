## 1. Lists on the ballot, and withdrawals

**38 lists were submitted (7–8 Sep) and approved by the CEC on 27 Sep 2026**, with ballot letters assigned.
- Letters: ynet, 27 Sep 2026: https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/sjmed8uqgx
- The approval is also reported by Srugim: https://www.srugim.co.il/101011998 (title: "ועדת הבחירות המרכזית: עדכון חשוב לציבור")
- Ra'am and the Joint List were approved only conditionally. On 1–2 Oct the Supreme Court overturned their disqualification and Ofer Cassif's. Sami Abu Shehadeh (Balad, No. 3 on the Joint List) withdrew personally. Source: Hebrew Wikipedia, citing Haaretz, 1 Oct.

### Withdrawals after submission (verified)

1. **Blue and White (כן).** Gantz announced on 19 Oct that he was withdrawing ([ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/hj00o26m2zl), [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/blue-and-whites-benny-gantz-pulls-out-of-race-sealing-precipitous-political-fall/)).
   - On 21 Oct the CEC announced that all candidates had resigned and the list will not run ([kikar](https://www.kikar.co.il/political-news/blue-white-withdraws-all-candidates-resign-ballots), [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/news/2752151/)).
   - Its ballots will not be placed in the booths. Any Blue and White ballot found in the count, including those already cast abroad and in IDF stations, is invalid.
   - **No endorsement.** Gantz said he would keep working for a national unity government. Eisenkot welcomed the move.
2. **Achi (צבי).** Announced its withdrawal on Fri 23 Oct after a deal with Likud ([ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/sypa7jfngl): "התמיכה במפלגה תעבור לליכוד"). In return, Netanyahu promised a deputy-minister role or arrangement.
   - On Sun 25 Oct the CEC announced that all candidates had resigned and the list is off the ballot ([kikar](https://www.kikar.co.il/news/tngz0z), [srugim](https://www.srugim.co.il/101014360)).
   - **Endorsement conflict:** on the evening of 25 Oct, Rabbi H.Y.D. Abergel publicly told followers to vote **Otzma Yehudit (ב)** ([hm-news](https://hm-news.co.il/664355/), [ch10](https://ch10.co.il/news/1109854/)). HM-News valued the endorsement at "about 10,000 votes", which is an estimate, not a measured figure.
3. **HaKahal (רץ, Shlomo Alboim).** The party announced overnight on 22→23 Oct that it would not run ([kikar](https://www.kikar.co.il/political-news/hakahal-party-withdraws-from-elections), 23 Oct 08:07). It cited the changes on UTJ's list and endorsed no one.
   - Israel Policy Forum and israeled.org also report the withdrawal.
   - **I found no CEC notice that its candidates resigned**, unlike the notices for Blue and White and Achi. Its ballots may still be in the booths. Its vote was negligible: it was not measured in polls.
4. **Orot HaShachar (בקר).** No. 1 Nissim Louk personally withdrew on 20 Oct and endorsed **Amcha Israel** (Hebrew Wikipedia, citing kipa 1234228-0; also bhirot26.online). **The list itself remains on the ballot.**

**Net result:** 36 lists are formally on the ballot (38 minus Blue and White and Achi), or effectively 35 if HaKahal's withdrawal is counted. ToI's guide of 19 Oct lists 37 parties, after Gantz but before Achi/HaKahal ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/everything-you-need-to-know-tois-guide-to-the-37-parties-that-israelis-can-finally-choose-from/)).

**No mergers between lists in the last two weeks.** Mergers can't happen after submission. The only changes were the withdrawals above.

Pre-submission mergers and dropouts, for context:
- Bennett + Yesh Atid formed "Together" (26 Apr)
- RZP + Zehut (1 Sep)
- Hendel + Zelicha (6 Sep)
- Erdan's "HaAchdut" dropped out (4 Sep)
- Several small lists did not file (8 Sep)

The full 38-list table with letters is in `lists_2026.csv`.

## 2. Surplus-vote agreements: official

## 3. Electorate size

- **Eligible voters, 26th Knesset: 7,331,981** (CEC figure). Sources: [Davar, 21 Oct](https://www.davar1.co.il/702908/), [Davar, 25 Oct](https://www.davar1.co.il/703523/), [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/local/article/21675114).
  - About 6.5M live in Israel and about 810k abroad.
  - The in-Israel electorate grew by about 333k (+5.4%) since 2022, per CBS.
  - CBS composition of eligible voters living in Israel: 77% Jewish, 18% Arab, 5% other. Haredim are about 10%.
  - *Conflict:* on 11 Oct, Sohlberg cited **7,336,553** ([walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/israel/383957015)). That is probably an earlier or interim register count. Use 7,331,981 as the official final figure.
- **Polling stations: 12,018**, of which 6,209 are accessible ([Davar, 25 Oct](https://www.davar1.co.il/703523/); walla, 11 Oct). About 450M ballots were printed.
- **Diplomatic missions (voted 15–16 Oct):** 4,218 voted, 86.7% turnout, versus 69.6% in 2022. Voting took place at 99 missions with 101 polling stations ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israeli-emissaries-abroad-end-voting-with-turnout-17-percentage-points-higher-than-2022/)). Wikipedia gives an eligible pool of about 4,800.
- **Soldiers / double envelopes 2026:** I found no published figures. For reference, about 463k double-envelope votes were cast in 2022, almost 10% of all votes ([walla, 25 Oct](https://www.walla.co.il/news/israel/383958794)).
- **2022 reference (25th Knesset):** 6,788,804 eligible voters ([INN](https://www.inn.co.il/news/581661)), with 12,495 polling stations.
  - Turnout was 70.62–70.63%. [ynet, 20 Oct](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/yokra14932317) gives 70.62%.
  - 4,794,593 votes / 6,788,804 = 70.625%, so the figure rounds to 70.63%. The 4,794,593 vote count is from my recollection and was not re-verified online. Your 70.63% figure is consistent with it.

## 4. Turnout signals for 2026

- **Arab sector:**
  - Stat-Net's Yousef Makladeh expects **68–69%**, versus 53.2% in 2022 and about 45% in 2021 ([Calcalist, 22 Oct](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/rjc2vdi3fx); the article's range is 60–70%).
  - Haaretz cites estimates "approaching 63%, potential 70%" (search snippet, 25 Oct).
  - An Abraham Initiatives / Afkar survey (June) put expected Arab turnout at about 60% (search snippet only; not fetched).
  - The Israel Policy Forum 120 Project (23 Oct) cites projections of Arab turnout **above 65%** ([IPF](https://israelpolicyforum.org/onetwenty/)).
- **Jewish sector:** IPF says "projections show as high as 80% turnout among Jewish voters", including tens of thousands flying in from abroad. It gives no primary source, so treat this as low confidence.
  - i24 says at least 50,000 Israelis abroad are expected to fly in (via [israeled.org, 23 Oct](https://israeled.org/israeli-election-watch-8/)).
- **Haredi turnout:** ynet (20 Oct) notes concern that Haredi turnout may fall, but gives no number.
- **Threshold scenarios:** at 70% turnout the threshold is about 167k votes, and at 75% about 178k ([ynet, 20 Oct](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/yokra14932317)). Channel 12/mako put it at 165,635 votes at 70%.
- **Undecided voters:** the last Times of Israel poll found 13% undecided (via israeled.org).
- **Not found:**
  - a 2026 IDI or aChord intention-to-vote figure
  - a Konrad Adenauer Program figure
  - any official CEC soldiers' early-vote count

## 5. Pages referenced by the competition

- **IDI 66233**, "אחוז החסימה והקולות המבוזבזים" (Ofer Kenig, 14 Sep 2026):
  - 2022 wasted votes were about **417,400**, nearly **9%** of valid votes, a record. April 2019 was about 8.5%.
  - Earlier wasted-vote shares: 1981 >5%, 1999 about 6%, 2013 >7%. 2020 and 2021 were the lowest since 1977.
  - Threshold history: 1% → 1.5% (early 1990s) → 2% (2006–2013) → **3.25% since 2015**.
  - Meretz missed the threshold in 2022 by **4,092 votes**.
  - In 2026, 38 lists are running, and polls give only **11–13** a realistic chance. The article finds no clear link between the threshold's height and the share of wasted votes.
- **IDI 66114**, Israeli Voice Index for August 2026 (published 8 Sep):
  - Fieldwork 31 Aug–3 Sep: 605 Hebrew-language and 150 Arabic-language respondents, ±3.57%.
  - **59%** expect improper interference by domestic political actors, and **51%** expect foreign interference.
  - 56% expect the losing side to refuse to accept the results.
  - Top vote factors: among Jews, security (39%) and the economy (38%); among Arabs, crime (53.5%) and the economy (46%).
  - 68% of Jews say 7 October is central to their vote.
  - **No turnout or intention-to-vote figure.** About 14% chose "other / don't know / not voting" as their main factor.
- **kolot-nodedim.netlify.app**, "קולות נודדים" by Harel Cohen (code: github harelc/elections-vote-transfer):
  - An interactive explorer of historical Knesset results across 11 Knessets, by list, locality, polling station, eligible voters and turnout. It models vote transfer between elections.
  - The landing page shows **no 2026-specific numbers**.

## 6. Late-campaign events (after about 18 Oct)

- **Gantz withdrew** (19 Oct). Blue and White had been polling below the threshold.
- **Achi withdrew** (23 Oct) in a deal with Likud, then its rabbi endorsed Otzma (25 Oct). Ynet reported on the Netanyahu–Abergel deal and the rabbi's sex-offence suspicions.
- **HaKahal withdrew** (23 Oct).
- **Orot HaShachar's leader** endorsed Amcha Israel (20 Oct).
- **Pressure on Winter (Amcha) and Leitner to quit.** Both refused.
- **High Court, 22 Oct, 2–1:** barred parties from real-time digital voter tracking at polling stations. This followed a 18 Oct affidavit about a Likud app.
- **Sohlberg ordered Likud to remove Arabic billboards** that tied Eisenkot to Abbas and Tibi (Hebrew Wikipedia, 22 Oct).
- **Violence and threats:**
  - death threats against Liberman (21 Oct)
  - Bennett's security doubled (21–22 Oct)
  - a bottle thrown at Netanyahu's motorcade (22 Oct)
  - a "wanted dead or alive" billboard defacement targeting Golan (19 Oct)
- **Trump openly endorsed Netanyahu** (IPF, 23 Oct). Polls suggested little effect.
- **Netanyahu posted an AI image** of a nuclear blast over Tel Aviv (25 Oct).
- **Poll blackout** from Fri 23 Oct.
- israeled.org estimates the three withdrawn lists could together have drawn up to about 2% of votes. This is an analyst estimate.

General background sources: English Wikipedia ("2026 Israeli legislative election", "Party lists for the 2026 Israeli legislative election") and Hebrew Wikipedia ("הבחירות לכנסת העשרים ושש"). Facts were cross-checked against the primary media and official links given above.
